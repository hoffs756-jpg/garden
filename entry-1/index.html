When to sow in New York

      

        
March to April
Start tomatoes, peppers and basil indoors on a bright windowsill. Sow peas and spinach outside once the soil thaws.

        
Mid May
After the last frost, move seedlings out and sow cucumbers, zinnias, sunflowers and nasturtiums straight into the pot.

        
August
Sow lettuce, cilantro and radishes again for a second harvest that lasts through the first frost in November.
